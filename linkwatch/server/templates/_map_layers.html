Layer 3: subnets

Each bubble is a subnet, with its VLAN when the interface is a VLAN interface. Routers, firewalls and layer 3 switches connect to every subnet they have an address in, and the link shows that address (the gateway). Monitored boxes whose address is in a subnet hang off it. Other hosts are counted on the bubble unless Every host is on. Addresses come from SNMP (the IP address table) and from pfSense, VyOS, PAN-OS, FortiGate and SonicWall.

Layer 2: VLANs

Each bubble is a VLAN, labelled with its name and number, with its subnet and SSIDs when known. Switches connect to the VLANs they carry, and the link names the access ports (or tagged for trunks). Routers and firewalls connect to the VLANs they route, access points to the VLANs their clients are on, and monitored hosts to the VLAN their address is on. VLANs come from SNMP (Q-BRIDGE-MIB, or Cisco VTP), from firewall VLAN interfaces, and from UniFi clients and SSIDs.
