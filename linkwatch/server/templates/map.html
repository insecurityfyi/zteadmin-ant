Network map

Built from LLDP and CDP neighbours polled over SNMP, integration uplinks (UniFi, Meraki), boxes seen on UniFi switch ports, and links drawn by hand. A box seen by more than one source (SNMP and an API, or under two names) is drawn once. Each link shows its speed; colour shows the busier direction's utilisation. Gateways connect to the Internet cloud, one line per WAN uplink. Drag to arrange, scroll to zoom, click a device to open it.
