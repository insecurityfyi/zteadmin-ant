Network map

Built from LLDP and CDP neighbours polled over SNMP, integration uplinks (UniFi, Meraki), boxes seen on UniFi switch ports, and links drawn by hand. A box seen by more than one source (SNMP and an API, or under two names) is drawn once. Each link shows its speed; colour shows the busier direction's utilisation. Each gateway WAN uplink runs to a circle for its ISP. Drag to arrange, scroll to zoom, click a device to open it.

Internet uplinks and ISPs

Each WAN is drawn to a circle for its ISP, with its speed and live throughput. Type the ISP for each WAN; WANs with the same name share a circle. Leave it empty to use the ISP found automatically: an IP range under Settings, what the gateway reports, or an online lookup of the WAN's public address. To add a WAN that is not listed, open its port and turn on Internet uplink.
